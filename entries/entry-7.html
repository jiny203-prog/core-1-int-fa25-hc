Nightmare I

I used to think nightmares ended when I woke up. But sometimes, they just shift shape — turn into mornings where I still can't move, where the air feels like it's made of sleep paralysis and guilt. Every time I close my eyes, the same corridor appears — flickering lights, walls too close. I walk and walk, and the sound of my steps never matches my pace. I dream of alarms that never stop ringing. I dream of my body floating above the bed, watching me breathe wrong. I dream that the ceiling melts into eyes. And when I finally wake up, I still see the outline of that figure at the door. Maybe insomnia isn't about not sleeping. Maybe it's about never truly waking up.
