Nightmare I

Eyes open.
Still dark.
A figure at the door.
Breathing that isn't mine.
Sheets tangled like veins.
I try to scream, but nothing leaves.
The clock says 3:17. Again.
The same second repeats.
Someone whispers: “You've been here before.”

Nightmare II

I dream of waking up.
The light spills wrong, sideways, too slow.
My heartbeat ticks louder than the clock.
The window is open but the curtain doesn't move.
I walk toward it and the floor softens, like breath.
Outside, the city is underwater.
I look down,
My reflection is standing where I was.
It smiles and closes the window.

Nightmare III

The air smells like static.
My phone rings, but it's my own voice on the line.
It asks where I've been.
I say, “Nowhere,” and the voice laughs.
The wallpaper peels like skin.
I cover my ears, but the silence grows louder.
Something scratches from inside the walls.

Nightmare IV

I wake up in water.
Ceiling lights ripple like stars.
My lungs forget what breathing means.
The floor hums, distant and alive.
I look down—my reflection blinks first.
It smiles, then sinks away.
I follow

Nightmare V

Someone knocks three times.
I count four.
My hands won't unclench.
The hallway stretches like a scream.
Every door leads back to this one.
I knock once,
And something knocks back.

Nightmare VI

I am running without sound.
My footsteps echo a beat too late.
The moon flickers like a dying bulb.
Every breath tastes metallic.
The trees whisper names I've never had.
A hand brushes my shoulder.
I turn—there's only my own shadow.

Nightmare VII

My body floats above my bed again.
Sheets ripple like thin ice.
I can see myself twitching below.
My mouth moves, but the sound lags.
I reach out—my fingers pass through my own face.
Something grabs my wrist.
I pull—and wake up gasping, still held.

Nightmare VIII

The room has no corners.
Every wall curves toward me.
I can't tell if I'm shrinking or if the air is swelling.
My name drips from the ceiling.
It lands on my skin and burns.
I try to wipe it away—
But it's carved there now.

Nightmare IX

The light turns red, then stops existing.
I hear the hum of electricity but nothing works.
Clocks melt into sound.
I count seconds that never end.
My eyes are open in two directions.
The sky folds inward.
I whisper: “Please not again.”

Nightmare X

It's morning, but the air still tastes like night.
My alarm rings—no sound, only vibration.
I walk to the mirror, and my reflection is still asleep.
I shake her shoulder.
She opens her eyes and says,
“Go back to bed. It's not over yet.”
I listen.
